NEPHROS – Technical Specification
Kidney Health Support

NEPHROS

Supports the normal functioning of the avian kidney system and helps maintain renal health in poultry birds.

Key Benefits
  • Supports proper kidney function in poultry
  • Helps maintain normal renal portal circulation
  • Improves metabolic waste elimination
  • Supports urinary balance and kidney health
  • Helps birds maintain overall physiological balance
Product Information

The avian kidneys are located on either side of the vertebral column and play a crucial role in removing metabolic waste and maintaining fluid balance in birds.

Application

Supports kidney health in broilers, layers and breeder birds.
