BOVITON – Technical Specification
Cattle Feed Supplement

BOVITON

High potency nutritional liquid formulation containing vitamins, minerals, and essential amino acids designed to improve dairy cattle productivity, lactation yield, and reproductive health.

Key Benefits
  • Promotes high milk yield and fat percentage
  • Boosts skeletal growth in calves and young animals
  • Strengthens immune resistance to common dairy diseases
  • Prevents pre- and post-partum ketosis
  • Supports peak lactation persistence
Application

Lactating cows, buffaloes, pregnant heifers, and growing calves.

Dosage

Cattle/Buffaloes: 50-100ml daily.
Calves: 20-30ml daily.

COLCURE – Technical Specification
Gut Health Support

COLCURE

A therapeutic antidiarrheal and gastrointestinal stabilizer formulated with natural astringents and micro-organism balance regulators to restore gut health in livestock.

Key Benefits
  • Fast-acting control of bacterial diarrhea and enteritis
  • Minimizes fluid loss and prevents dehydration
  • Restores rumen pH and active microflora
  • Soothes the intestinal lining from toxin irritation
  • Promotes rapid recovery of appetite
Application

Calves, cattle, sheep, and goats with digestive disorders.

Dosage

Cattle: 100-150g daily.
Calves/Sheep: 20-30g daily for 3 days.

CALCIUM-MAX – Technical Specification
Mineral Care Supplement

CALCIUM-MAX

An ionic liquid calcium supplement enriched with Vitamin D3, Vitamin B12, and galactagogue herbs. Formulated specifically to optimize calcium levels during critical calving periods.

Key Benefits
  • Highly bio-available calcium prevents milk fever
  • Supports strong bone structure and skeletal density
  • Galactagogue herbs stimulate natural milk secretion
  • Enhances uterus involution after calving
  • Supports neuromuscular and cardiac function
Application

High-yielding dairy cattle pre- and post-calving.

Dosage

100ml twice daily or as recommended by a veterinarian.

BOVIPRO – Technical Specification
Bovine Protein Supplement

BOVIPRO

A premium protein concentrate containing bypass protein, amino acids, and rumen-protected methionine/lysine. Formulated to enhance protein supply and milk SNF (Solid Not Fat) percentage.

Key Benefits
  • Improves milk fat and SNF percentages significantly
  • Promotes daily weight gain in meat cattle
  • Improves digestion of fiber and low-quality roughage
  • Enhances metabolic energy efficiency
  • Shortens the calving interval
Application

Growing beef cattle, lactating cows, and bulls.

Dosage

50-100g mixed with daily grain feed.

RUMEN-BOOST – Technical Specification
Digestant Feed Additive

RUMEN-BOOST

An active digestant powder containing live yeast cultures, probiotics, enzymes, and cobalt. Designed to stabilize rumen pH and treat indigestion.

Key Benefits
  • Prevents and treats rumen acidosis and bloat
  • Stimulates cellulose-digesting bacteria
  • Re-establishes normal feed intake post-antibiotic treatment
  • Enhances forage digestibility and nutrient absorption
  • Eliminates toxins from the digestive tract
Application

Indigestion, anorexia, bloat, and rumen acidosis.

Dosage

Adult Cattle: 15-20g daily.
Sheep/Goats: 5-10g daily.

MAMMI-SHIELD – Technical Specification
Udder Health Support

MAMMI-SHIELD

Advanced prophylactic formula containing vitamins, minerals, and herbal extracts to support teat tissue integrity, improve milk quality, and prevent mastitis.

Key Benefits
  • Significantly reduces Somatic Cell Count (SCC) in milk
  • Builds a protective physiological barrier against mastitis
  • Stimulates tissue regeneration in the mammary gland
  • Restores milk production after clinical mastitis recovery
  • Keeps teat skin smooth, supple, and crack-free
Application

High-yield dairy cows during early and mid-lactation.

Dosage

Cattle/Buffaloes: 50g daily for 4-6 days.
